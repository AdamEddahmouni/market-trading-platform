## Qualification and accounting

Complete pinned enumeration and the existing deterministic eligibility classifier precede
one frozen canonical input per eligible instrument. SCREENER_LOCAL_QUALIFICATION evaluates
US equities and ETFs separately. Futures, bonds and crypto are explicitly accounted and block
staged selection. Identity-based order is independent of list rank.

WARRANTS_REVIEW means admitted evidence warrants further operator interpretation.
NO_SUPPORTED_CASE means a resolved, grounded absence of a material review case.
UNCERTAIN preserves ambiguity, contradiction or insufficient interpretation. Missing optional
news, flow or sentiment alone cannot justify exclusion. Price, volume, symbol, list position
and sentiment are not standalone qualification rules. There are no probability or return claims.

Responses have exactly schema version, instrument identity, category, rationale, supporting
references, conflicting references and categorical uncertainties. Server metadata adds assessment
identity, engine/runtime, evidence hash, cutoff, missing capabilities, validation and elapsed time.
Resolved outputs require a strong current quote and another strong owned observation.
Malformed, extra-field, foreign-identity/reference, weak-support, prohibited-claim or inconsistent
capability outputs retain raw failures. No judgment repair or preferred-answer retry occurs.
Mechanical validation establishes compliance; semantic grounding requires independent research.

Only a valid NO_SUPPORTED_CASE does not advance. Warranted, uncertain and failed assessments
remain in the entire premium pool. No arbitrary cap applies. Local failures prevent a complete
local-coverage claim. A complete empty selection requires all eligible local assessments to be
valid and resolved. Exclusive primary states reconcile to the full universe; derived counters
overlap and carry their basis explicitly.

Source rows, quotes, admitted news, conflicts, provenance and expiry are revalidated before
premium, after responses and at terminal publication. Changed material evidence invalidates
qualification. Neither processing nor reuse renews original validity. Frozen historical
snapshots remain isolated replay evidence, never current-market authority.

## Premium, persistence and authority

Pin the configured hosted provider at run start and verify both configuration and returned
identity. Refuse unavailable/incompatible/changed engines without substitution. Reuse existing
strict candidate-local output, context checks, batching, shared quota holds and global 0–5
reduction. Plan the whole pool, output/reasoning allowance, worst-case comparisons and drift
margin before generation. Oversized pools issue zero premium generations, with available
allowance and shortfall. Dollar estimates require supported configured prices.

Immutable ai_screener_experimental_results and ai_screener_staged_records use existing
local-state transactions. They never publish operational candidate_run records. Default latest
reads remain exhaustive; experimental latest and interruption receipts remain separately
account scoped and restart discoverable. Action Decision rejects experimental identity and
forged approval before preview, inference or handoff. Experimental UI results expose no
decision or Paper control. Fixture reauthorization exists only under tests and isolated stores.

Stop checks occur before local dispatch, after queue wait/startup and before each premium call.
Returned in-flight outcomes retain usage even when rejected. Restart never automatically resumes
paid work. Reuse namespaces include stage/method, model/runtime, prompt/schema, canonical evidence,
cutoff and comparison context. Every hit revalidates; corrupt local cached judgments fail without
duplicate inference.

## Frozen research and acceptance

[Manifest](../../artifacts/ai-screener-local-first/manifest.json) froze 52 controlled cases
before real output inspection: two universes, thirteen adverse archetypes, separate development
and holdout identities, hashes, cutoffs, labels, exclusions, ties, metrics, prompts/config and
repeat/order/format perturbations. Shared synthetic templates limit independent generalization.
There was no holdout tuning.

Research targets: top-5 reference recall 100%; labeled-positive recall at least 95%; invalid
references accepted zero; invalid outputs at most 1%; disagreement at most 5%; estimated
paid-token reduction at least 90% while fitting unchanged allowance. They grant no approval.
Matching real premium records and exact-context replay are unavailable; October 7 receipts are
software controls. Real quality denominators are zero. Recall, precision, false exclusions,
selection agreement, rank correlation and real premium batch/tail sensitivity are unavailable.

[Engineering scales](../../artifacts/ai-screener-local-first/engineering-scales.json) reconcile
20, 50, 100, 500, 4,630 and 20,000 rows with fixture engines and isolated stores. They establish
orchestration, not quality or achieved economic savings. Real scales remain resource blocked.
The cumulative two-hour local inference ceiling reserves 720 seconds per admitted pipeline
for worst-case queue/startup/template/tokenization/generation. Dispatch and returned-response
counts are separate from validity.

An initial harness mislabeled resource-admission loops as measurements; the attempts summary
and corrected classifications are preserved as historical diagnostics, and the raw receipt
archive is retained outside the repository with its SHA-256 in the acceptance receipt. Their times
are not model latency. The old diagnostic CPU-selection artifact is not a backend winner.

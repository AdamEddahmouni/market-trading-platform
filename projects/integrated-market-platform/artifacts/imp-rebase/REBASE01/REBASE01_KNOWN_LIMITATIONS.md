# IMP-REBASE-01 known limitations

## Current program limitations documented by REBASE-01

1. A universal append-only run and artifact ledger, common attempt/disposition
   model, and program-wide workflow/control registry do not yet exist.
2. End-to-end trace semantics and an accepted performance benchmark spanning
   provider, platform, feature, model, risk, UI, human, and broker stages are
   incomplete.
3. Operating Fabric, Real-Time Opportunity Fabric, Cross-Asset,
   Narrative/Motive, and AI/Agents are `PARTIAL` consolidation families.
4. EVIDENCE-01C has not produced an accepted bounded real-provider shakedown and
   operational-acceptance record.
5. Accepted production live broker transport and operationally accepted live
   execution are absent. Existing broker abstractions, paper/mock paths,
   live-safety gates, authorization, confirmation, and reconciliation
   foundations do not close that limitation.
6. Automatic documentation metadata, drift, contradiction, current-reference,
   and repository-wide link validation are future standards, not implemented
   REBASE-01 automation.

These limitations belong to the broader program and do not make the
documentation-only REBASE-01 milestone incomplete.

## Limitations of REBASE-01 execution

None.

# Catalyst Evidence Rule Semantics

Catalyst rules report objective presence and timing only. News must be active, have an eligible
publication/availability boundary, and remain within provider scope. Later or withdrawn news
cannot support a pre-boundary rule. Unknown publication time remains `UNKNOWN`. SEC filings use
their public acceptance lifecycle. Corporate actions, including reverse splits, are descriptive
context only.

No rule interprets a headline as positive or negative, performs sentiment inference, or claims
causation.

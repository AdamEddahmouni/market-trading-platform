## Decision rule

Every future adoption requires an accepted ADR, a new implementation authority,
provenance and rights evidence, point-in-time tests where relevant, deterministic
offline replay, and proof that forecast, strategy, risk, execution, and
accounting boundaries remain separate. Absence of a row is not permission.
